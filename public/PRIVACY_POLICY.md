# Graceward Privacy Policy

**Effective: August 10, 2026**

Graceward is operated by Cosmiq LLC. This policy explains how data is handled when you use the app.

We process account details, app settings, completed daily practices, reflections you choose to save, subscription status, device and notification information, support messages, and limited technical diagnostics. For daily encouragements, this can include when an encouragement is received or opened, whether playback starts, playback milestones, whether it is heard substantially, replay count, and the last interaction time. We also record limited first-party journey events, such as whether a focus was selected or a connected practice was completed, without putting user-authored message or reflection text in those event properties. Spiritual reflections can be sensitive; enter only what you are comfortable storing and processing.

We use this information to authenticate accounts, sync completed practices, maintain your private daily encouragement history so you can revisit what you received and heard, show Scripture and prayer content, deliver reminders, provide requested AI-assisted reflections, process subscriptions, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve reliability. If Companion memory is enabled, recent daily patterns and durable details you choose to share in Companion chat may personalize later Companion replies. You can turn this off or clear chat-learned personal memory in Account & settings without deleting conversations or earned progress. We do not use completion or listening data to judge holiness, salvation, God’s favor, or spiritual worth.

Graceward uses service providers such as Supabase for application infrastructure and, when an AI feature is requested, OpenAI for language-model processing. Apple or another store processes in-app purchases. Providers process data under their own terms and our service arrangements. Do not put information in an AI prompt that you would not want transmitted for processing.

We use reasonable safeguards and retain information only as needed for the service, legal obligations, security, and dispute resolution. No system can guarantee absolute security.

You can review settings in the app and request account deletion. Account deletion is permanent for app data, subject to limited legal or backup retention, and does not itself cancel an App Store subscription. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or object to processing.

Graceward is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy and will post the revised effective date.
