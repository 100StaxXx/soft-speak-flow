# Cosmiq Privacy Policy

**Effective: August 18, 2026**

Cosmiq is operated by Cosmiq LLC. This policy explains how data is handled when you use the app.

We may process account details, onboarding and Guide preferences, plans, goals, actions, habits, check-ins, reflections, companion choices and progress, support messages, subscription status, device and notification information, feature usage, and limited technical diagnostics.

We use this information to authenticate your Cosmiq account; sync your saved plans and progress; personalize requested Guide, planning, reflection, and companion experiences; deliver enabled reminders; process subscriptions; prevent abuse; provide support; troubleshoot problems; and improve reliability.

Requested AI features may send relevant prompt and context to an AI service provider, including OpenAI. Cosmiq also uses providers such as Supabase for application infrastructure and Apple or another store for purchase processing. Do not enter information you would not want transmitted to these providers for processing. We do not sell personal data.

We use reasonable safeguards and retain information as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, protect security, resolve disputes, and complete backup rotation. No system can guarantee absolute security.

You can manage settings, disable notifications in device controls, disconnect optional integrations, and permanently delete your Cosmiq account and saved app data. Account deletion does not itself cancel an App Store subscription. Additional privacy rights may apply where you live.

Cosmiq is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy and will post a revised effective date.
