# Cosmiq Feature List And Product Brochure

## One-Sentence Positioning

Cosmiq is a fantasy productivity and personal growth companion that turns real life into quests, campaigns, rituals, mentor conversations, and companion evolution.

## Product Thesis

Most productivity apps ask users to become more mechanical. Cosmiq asks them to become more alive.

The app frames planning, follow-through, self-reflection, and habit change as an ongoing fantasy journey. The user does not simply maintain a task list. They hatch a companion, choose or get matched with a guide, build campaigns around meaningful goals, turn responsibilities into quests, and earn progress through action.

Cosmiq wants to be the daily growth layer between a person's intentions and their actual day. It should help users decide what matters, recover when the day breaks, build repeatable rituals, feel emotionally supported, and see their growth reflected back through story, art, audio, rewards, and companion evolution.

## What Cosmiq Wants To Be

Cosmiq wants to become the user's living productivity world:

- A task manager that feels like a quest log.
- A habit tracker that feels like ritual training.
- A goal planner that feels like a campaign map.
- A coach that feels like a mentor, not a dashboard.
- A companion that grows because the user grows.
- A reflection system that turns hard days into usable wisdom.
- A playful fantasy shell around serious self-leadership.

The long-term ambition is not just "gamified productivity." It is a personal operating system with mythic emotional texture: a place where ambition, discipline, softness, recovery, creativity, and accountability can all live together.

## Core Audience

Cosmiq is for people who want to improve their lives but do not want another sterile checklist.

Primary user types:

- People who enjoy games, fantasy, RPG progression, companions, collections, and story unlocks.
- People who struggle with consistency and need emotional buy-in, not just reminders.
- People with big goals who need those goals broken into daily actions.
- People who want coaching but prefer a softer, more imaginative container.
- People who are tired of productivity apps that make them feel behind.
- People who like personalization: different mentors, different tones, different companion identities, different planning modes.

## Product Pillars

### 1. Fantasy Productivity

Cosmiq rebrands ordinary productivity language into a more motivating fantasy framework:

- Tasks become quests.
- Long-term goals become campaigns.
- Habits become rituals.
- Progress becomes XP.
- Milestones become postcards, story beats, and evolution moments.
- Planning becomes guidance from a companion or mentor.
- Recovery becomes part of the journey instead of failure.

### 2. Living Companion

The companion is the emotional anchor of the app. It is not just an avatar. It grows, talks, remembers, evolves, appears in planning surfaces, reacts to progress, and becomes a visual representation of the user's consistency.

### 3. Human Mentor Energy

The mentor layer gives users a chosen guide with personality, voice, tone, and coaching style. The app supports different kinds of encouragement: calm, direct, strategic, aspirational, playful, intense, or identity-building.

### 4. Practical Planning

The fantasy layer is only useful if it produces real-world clarity. Cosmiq includes concrete daily planning, schedules, calendar integration, reminders, recurring rituals, subtasks, attachments, duration estimates, and AI-assisted restructuring.

### 5. Recovery-First Growth

The app is designed around real days, not ideal days. Low energy, missed quests, overloaded schedules, drifting campaigns, and broken streaks are treated as conditions to adapt around.

### 6. Story And Reward Loops

Cosmiq keeps growth emotionally legible with progression tiers, badges, postcards, companion stories, weekly recaps, pep talks, collections, and milestone celebrations.
