# Badge artwork — built-in image generation

Seven category illustrations generated with the imagegen skill and built-in tool; reviewed at full size, resized to 512px WebP for the app. Badge titles and tier labels remain individual and accessible. No per-user generation cost.

## streaks

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Cosmiq mobile achievement badge illustration (streaks)
Primary request: One premium collectible badge: a graceful sculpted flame with three ascending sparks, signifying consistency.
Style/medium: refined dimensional fantasy game inventory art, crisp enamel and softly brushed silver, subtle violet and icy teal accents, tasteful delicate engraved details. Restrained luminous highlights, no glaring neon.
Composition/framing: single centered emblem, square image, entire silhouette visible with generous clean margin; recognizable at 64 pixels. Soft near-black charcoal background (#10171c), not an orange rounded tile.
Constraints: no words, letters, numbers, ribbons, watermark or extra objects. Finished production artwork, not a placeholder.

Asset: public/badges-v2/streaks.webp
