# Cosmiq Brochure

## Cover

Cosmiq

Fantasy productivity for real life.

## The Idea

Cosmiq is for people who want to grow, but do not want their life reduced to a sterile checklist.

It turns planning, habits, reflection, and long-term goals into a living fantasy journey. Your tasks become quests. Your habits become rituals. Your goals become campaigns. Your progress feeds an evolving companion that grows as you do.

## What You Do In Cosmiq

Plan your day with help from an AI companion.

Ask what matters, what to do next, what is coming up, or how to recover when the day breaks.

Build campaigns from big goals.

Pathfinder helps turn ambition into rituals, milestones, deadlines, and a practical schedule.

Reflect and review.

Morning briefings, evening reflections, and weekly recaps help you understand your patterns and carry lessons forward.

Grow with a companion.

Choose an egg, element, species, name, story tone, and companion personality mode. Earn XP through real action and watch your companion evolve.

Choose your guide.

Mentors bring different coaching energies: calm, direct, strategic, intense, playful, disciplined, or identity-building.

## Product Pillars

### Quest-Based Planning

Cosmiq makes daily planning feel active and alive. Create quests with dates, times, durations, difficulties, subtasks, locations, reminders, attachments, and calendar send options.

### Campaigns And Rituals

Long-term goals become campaigns. Each campaign can include daily or custom rituals, phases, milestones, postcard moments, story themes, and progress trails.

### AI Companion Planner

The companion can plan the day, adjust the day, make room, prepare tomorrow, identify the next best action, surface campaign momentum, and protect the user from overloaded schedules.

### Mentors And Reflection

Guides provide daily encouragement, pep talks, chat, morning briefings, and practical follow-up. Reflection turns experience into useful insight instead of guilt.

### Companion Evolution

The companion is the emotional heart of the app. It hatches, levels, evolves, speaks, remembers, and appears across the planning experience as proof of the user's momentum.

### Rewards And Story

XP, badges, streaks, daily missions, weekly recaps, postcards, companion stories, collections, and astral encounters make growth visible and memorable.

## A Day In Cosmiq

1. Open the app and get a mentor briefing.
2. Ask the companion to plan the day.
3. Choose the quests that matter.
4. Protect time on the calendar.
5. Complete quests and rituals.
6. Ask "what should I do right now?" when stuck.
7. Adjust the day when energy or circumstances change.
8. Reflect at night.
9. Watch progress turn into XP, stories, badges, and companion evolution.
