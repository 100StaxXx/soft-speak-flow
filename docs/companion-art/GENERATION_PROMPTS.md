# Graceward eight-form reference prompt set

These references were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Each
animal used its canonical image in `src/assets/companions/symbolic/` as the sole
visual reference.

## Shared prompt contract

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Graceward biblical-symbolic companion eight-form production reference sheet
Primary request: Create one polished landscape sheet showing exactly eight chronological forms of the exact same companion from the reference: Beginning, Young, Growing, Rooted, Steady, Flourishing, Majestic, and Grand.
Style/medium: Match the supplied premium warm 2D mobile-game illustration exactly—clean linework, soft painterly shading, readable silhouette, family-friendly, emotionally expressive.
Composition/framing: Eight equal vertical panels in one row, animal fully visible in a consistent three-quarter view, evenly spaced, no overlap or cropping; preserve the same face, eye color, palette, and markings throughout.
Backdrop: Subtle warm woodland/parchment production board with quiet dividers, no UI and no text.
Growth language: Progress through natural age, posture, confidence, surface detail, and widening dawn light. Never change animal lineage or body plan.
Mood: Grace, courage, hope, faithful return, and companionship.
Avoid: Text, labels, weapons, occult symbols, cosmic imagery, extra animals, watermark, humanoid anatomy, armor, halo, crown, or deity imagery.
```

## Species prompt locks

### Lamb

Natural domestic sheep in every panel: exactly four legs with cloven hooves,
dense white wool, broad sheep ears, a gentle short sheep muzzle, warm brown
eyes, and a short tail. No wings, paws, predator teeth, antlers, fantasy horns,
or human face. Even at Grand it remains a gentle natural sheep companion.

### Lion

Natural African lion in every panel: exactly four feline legs and paws, one
broad feline head, rounded ears, amber eyes, one tufted tail, and an
age-appropriate natural mane. No wings, feathers, eagle beak, canine muzzle,
hooves, reptile scales, or human hands. Even at Grand it remains a calm natural
lion companion.

### Stag

Natural red deer in every panel: exactly four long deer legs with cloven
hooves, one long deer face, large alert ears, dark gentle eyes, one short deer
tail, and symmetrical age-appropriate branching antlers. Begin without antlers,
then show buds and biologically sensible tine growth. No wings, horse head,
horse hooves, predator paws, spiral fantasy horns, or extra antlers.

### Dove

Natural dove in every panel: exactly two pink legs, two feathered wings, one
compact dove head, one small pale beak, round warm-dark eyes, a compact breast,
and one short fan tail. No four legs, hawk talons, hooked eagle beak, mammal
muzzle, human face, or giant mount proportions. Even at Grand it remains a
natural white dove.

### Eagle

Natural eagle in every panel: exactly two taloned legs, two feathered wings,
one raptor head, one strong hooked beak, keen amber eyes, layered brown-gold
feathers, and one feathered tail. No four legs, lion body, mammal muzzle, human
face, human arms, or giant mount proportions. Even at Grand it remains a
natural eagle, never a griffin.

### Wolf

Natural grey wolf in every panel: exactly four digitigrade canine legs and
paws, one long wolf muzzle, erect triangular ears, amber eyes, a thick neck
ruff, and one bushy tail. No wings, horns, scales, cat muzzle, human hands,
humanoid posture, clothing, or extra tails. Even at Grand it remains a natural
wolf, never a werewolf or dog breed.
