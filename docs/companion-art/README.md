# Graceward biblical companion art direction

Graceward's active companion roster is limited to six biblical-symbolic animals:

- Lamb — gentle trust
- Lion — steadfast courage
- Stag — renewal and seeking
- Dove — peace and hope
- Eagle — vision and strength
- Wolf — faithful endurance

The fantasy preset catalog is legacy compatibility data. It must not appear in
new Graceward companion selection, marketing, story generation, or new art
production.

## Eight-form growth language

Every animal follows the same form sequence: Beginning, Young, Growing,
Rooted, Steady, Flourishing, Majestic, and Grand. Growth changes age,
confidence, posture, surface detail, and environmental light. It never changes
the animal's body plan or implies that the companion is sacred, divine, or a
source of spiritual authority.

The six `*-eight-form-reference-v1.png` sheets in this folder are the approved
visual starting point for those form boundaries. They are production reference
sheets, not runtime sprite atlases. Runtime portraits remain user-specific and
are generated at form boundaries from the canonical portrait plus the previous
approved evolution image.

## Required interactive pose set

Each form should eventually have the following species-faithful rig states or
pose variants:

1. neutral breathing
2. look left/right
3. blink and listen
4. greeting
5. head-touch response
6. chest/side touch response
7. play invitation
8. happy celebration
9. concerned/comforting
10. sleepy/settled
11. species signature
12. rare evolution entrance

The app can already drive these states through motion, gaze, touch zones,
questions, Guide events, and daily-practice events. Layered character rigs or
pose-specific portraits can replace the current whole-portrait motion without
changing the interaction contract.

## Species locks

- Lamb: four sheep legs, cloven hooves, wool, broad ears; no wings or predator features.
- Lion: four feline legs and paws, broad muzzle, mane, tufted tail; no wings or bird features.
- Stag: four deer legs, cloven hooves, deer face, symmetrical age-appropriate antlers.
- Dove: two legs, two wings, compact body, small pale beak; never a raptor.
- Eagle: two taloned legs, two wings, hooked beak, layered raptor feathers; never a griffin.
- Wolf: four canine legs and paws, long muzzle, erect ears, ruff, bushy tail; never humanoid.

Element and visual-nature treatments belong in palette, markings, atmosphere,
and light. They must not add horns, wings, limbs, human anatomy, or hybridize
the animal.
