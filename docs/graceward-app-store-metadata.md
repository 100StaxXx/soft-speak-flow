## Description

Graceward helps you bring faith into the day you actually have.

Begin with reviewed Scripture, a grounded reflection, and a short prayer. Receive one small practice already chosen for the day, then return in the evening for reflection without pressure or spiritual scorekeeping.

YOUR DAILY PRACTICE

• Read a reviewed daily Scripture passage
• Begin and end the day with prayer and reflection
• Receive one small practice each day—no goals or schedules to build
• Grow across faith, mind, body, relationships, stewardship, service, and rest
• Review your week with honesty and grace

MEET YOUR GUIDES

Choose an original fictional Guide whose communication style fits the way you want to reflect. Guides offer encouragement, questions, and practical next steps. They are AI-assisted software profiles—not pastors, clergy, historical religious figures, or spiritual authorities.

GROUNDED CHRISTIAN GUIDANCE

Graceward never claims to speak for God or provide divine revelation. It does not replace Scripture in context, prayer, Christian community, pastoral care, therapy, medical care, or emergency help.

PREMIUM

Graceward Plus adds deeper guided reflection, ready-made daily practices, Guide encouragement, and offline content. Subscription terms and pricing are shown before purchase and can be managed through your App Store account.
